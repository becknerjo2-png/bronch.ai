What is Bronchitis?

Bronchitis is inflammation of the bronchial tubes that carry air to and from the lungs. It can be acute or chronic and often causes coughing, mucus production, and breathing discomfort.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent cough
  • Mucus production
  • Shortness of breath
  • Wheezing
  • Chest discomfort
  • Fatigue

Prevention

  • Avoid smoking
  • Wash your hands frequently
  • Get recommended vaccinations
  • Exercise regularly
  • Maintain good indoor air quality

Treatment

Treatment depends on the cause and severity. Rest, fluids, humidification, and medical evaluation are important. Seek medical care if symptoms worsen or breathing becomes difficult.
